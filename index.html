Setup notes

Setup

  • Digital Wingspan (Steam), 2 players at one computer, Oceania player mats.
  • Expansions: base game, Swift Start promo pack, European, Oceania, Asia.
  • Fan packs: British Birds, Additional Asian Avians, Birds of Continental Europe.
  • Deck order stacked; dice rolls assumed favourable.

The Drongo engine

  • Scenario shared by another player. A (scoring) moves first and builds a forest of Greater Racket-Tailed Drongo, Northern Mockingbird, Gray Catbird, Tūī and Superb Lyrebird. S (support) has Savi's Warbler and Black Noddy in its wetland, Grey Shrikethrush and Great Eared Nightjar in its forest.
  • The Drongo tucks a bird with a brown power from A's hand on every activation and may copy that power; the Mockingbird and Catbird repeat the Drongo. Goldcrest and Short-Toed Treecreeper copies play extra forest birds, Black-Shouldered Kite and Green Pheasant copies lay eggs, feeder birds and Scissor-Tailed Flycatcher gain food, Greater Flamingo trades cards, and a Kelp Gull copy turns food into cards.
  • While the deck is being cleaned, A's Tūī and Lyrebird copy S's Grey Shrikethrush (all rodents in the feeder) and the Mockingbird and Catbird repeat them. The food goes to the Kelp Gull copy, which draws the rest of the non-forest birds.
  • Then the copiers copy S's Nightjar and the repeaters repeat the copiers: every A food turn runs the Nightjar 4 times. The Drongo keeps tucking a spare bird from hand without copying it.
  • S's Black Noddy resets the feeder and tucks a card from the deck for each fish discarded; Savi's Warbler draws for S and shares one card with A.
  • The movers fly between the forests and wetlands on both players' actions, ending in A's wetland.
  • The feeder is assumed to show what each reset needs.

How the digital game resolves things

  • Shared cards: when a power lets the other player draw too (for example "all players draw 1"), the active player first draws and resolves all of its own powers; the other player draws its share at the start of its own next turn, or as the round ends if that comes first.
  • Great Eared Nightjar: "draw cards from the deck until you draw a non-forest bird". Read literally, on a deck of only forest birds the run would never end. In the digital game:
    • If the deck runs out during the run and the discard pile has cards, they are shuffled in as the deck and the run goes on.
    • If the deck and the discard pile are both empty, the run stops. The drawn cards haven't been discarded yet: they are discarded as the last step of the Nightjar's power, and the other forest powers resolve after it.
    So each run caches one food for every forest bird in the deck and the discard pile.
